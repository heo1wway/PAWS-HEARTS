Adoption Fee

Dogs: $150-$300 · Cats: $75-$150 · Small Animals: $25-$75. All fees include spay/neuter, vaccinations, and microchipping.

What's Included

Every adopted pet comes fully vaccinated, microchipped, spayed or neutered, and with a free 30-day vet check-up.

Post-Adoption Support

Our team is available by phone and email to answer questions, offer guidance, and help you settle in with your new family member.

PAWS & HEARTS Animal Shelter

Since 1998, Paws & Hearts has been the region's most trusted
animal shelter — rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming
thousands of animals each year. Every pet deserves a loving
home.
