OUR STORY

About Paws & Hearts

For over 26 years, we've been Springfield's sanctuary for animals in need — a place built on love,
driven by compassion, and powered by community.

OUR MISSION

We Exist to Give Every Animal a Second Chance

Paws & Hearts was founded on a simple but powerful belief: no animal should suffer from neglect, abandonment, or cruelty. We provide a safe, loving environment for dogs, cats, rabbits, and small animals while we find them their perfect forever family.

From emergency rescues to long-term care, behavioral rehabilitation to community education — everything we do is centered on the well-being of animals and the humans who love them.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Core Values

Compassion First

Every decision we make is guided by genuine love and care for animals. We treat each pet as a valued individual with unique needs.

Safe & Trusted

Licensed, regulated, and transparent — we maintain the highest standards so every adopter and animal is protected.

Community Rooted

We grow with our community through outreach, education, and partnerships that make Springfield a more humane city.

OUR JOURNEY

26 Years of Compassion

1998

Paws & Hearts founded by Dr. Angela Morris with just 12 animals.

2003

Expanded to a new 5,000 sq ft facility with medical care on-site.

2010

Launched our foster-home network, doubling our rescue capacity.

2016

Reached 5,000 successful adoptions. Celebrated with the community.

2021

Opened the outdoor exercise yard and cat habitat tower.

2026

12,400+ rescues and still going strong. Thank you, Springfield!
